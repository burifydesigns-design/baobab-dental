# Burify sales demo guide — Baobab Dental

## Positioning

Baobab Dental is a fictional multi-location dental-clinic concept built to demonstrate how Burify can improve a clinic's digital patient journey. It is a demonstration asset, not a real clinic or a customer case study.

## What to show a prospective clinic

1. Start on the public homepage and show the primary appointment enquiry action.
2. Walk through treatments, clinic locations, team profiles, patient information, journal and contact options.
3. Submit a clearly labelled demo appointment request using non-personal test details. When the MongoDB environment is configured, it is stored as a pending request—not a confirmed appointment.
4. Sign in to the protected admin area and show the submitted request, patient contact details, preferred appointment details and status workflow.
5. Show that request status changes persist to MongoDB and are recorded in the admin audit log.
6. Be explicit that staff profiles, branch details, testimonials, prices and finance figures are illustrative. Only appointment requests are connected to MongoDB in this iteration.

## Honest sales language

Use: "This is a working design and workflow demonstration. The appointment enquiry path and admin request workflow can be connected to a database; the final integrations, privacy controls and practice-management connections are scoped around your clinic."

Do not use: "This clinic uses our system", "these are real patient bookings", "we increased bookings by X%", or any invented client outcome.

## Suggested conversation

"Your website should make it easy for patients to understand your services, choose the right clinic and send an enquiry. The admin side should help your team respond consistently. This demo shows the public enquiry flow and a protected request-management workflow. We would tailor the final experience, data handling and any practice-management integration to your clinic."
