# Burify sales demo guide — Baobab Dental

## Positioning

Baobab Dental is a fictional multi-location dental-clinic concept built to demonstrate how Burify can improve a clinic's digital patient journey. It is a demonstration asset, not a real clinic or a customer case study.

## What to show a prospective clinic

1. Start on the public homepage and show the primary appointment enquiry action.
2. Walk through treatments, clinic locations, team profiles, patient information and contact options.
3. Explain the appointment request journey: patients provide contact details and a preferred time; the clinic still confirms availability.
4. Open the admin demo and show appointment triage, staff allocation, clinic overview and finance visualisation.
5. Be explicit that sample staff, branch details, testimonials, prices and finance figures are illustrative.
6. Explain that the MongoDB API foundation stores consented appointment requests and protects admin API routes. The existing dashboard still needs to be connected to those endpoints before it can be represented as a live clinic system.

## Honest sales language

Use: "This is a working design and workflow demonstration. We can tailor the public website and connect an agreed admin workflow to your clinic's systems."

Do not use: "This clinic uses our system", "these are real patient bookings", "we increased bookings by X%", or any invented client outcome.

## Suggested conversation

"Your website should make it easy for patients to understand your services, choose the right clinic and send an enquiry. The admin side should help your team respond consistently. This demo shows the proposed experience; the final integrations, data model and privacy controls are scoped around your practice."
